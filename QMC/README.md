# Quasi-Monte Carlo (QMC)

## MATLAB Source Programs

MATLAB programs from the book *Handbook of Monte Carlo Methods* by Kroese,
Taimre, and Botev (2011), Wiley Series in Prob and Stat.

The MATLAB programs were downloaded from the
[Handbook of Monte Carlo Methods repository](https://github.com/burakbayramli/books/tree/master/Handbook_of_Monte_Carlo_Methods_Taimre_Kroese).
